Our Story

It started with three words, and it turned into forever.

Three words. One legendary beginning.

It all started with a simple message: “Hey, wassup, hello.”

Not exactly the most polished pickup line, but it was enough to start a conversation. That conversation quickly turned into laughs, late night talks, and the kind of connection that makes hours pass without either of us noticing.

Somewhere between the jokes, the stories, and the easy way everything seemed to fall into place, we realized this wasn’t just a great conversation. It was the beginning of something real.

Now we’re here, excited for the next chapter and grateful to celebrate it with the people who mean the most to us.
