Our Story

It started with three words, and it turned into forever.

Three words. One legendary beginning.

It all started with three words: “hey, wassup, hello.” Not poetry. Not a grand romantic speech. Just three little words that somehow landed like a movie trailer.

“Hey” was the warm up. “Wassup” was the confidence. “Hello” was the smooth reset, because apparently we were both committed to making sure the first impression happened at least three times.

After that came a lot of laughing, a little flirting, and the kind of conversations that accidentally turn into “Wait… what time is it?” and then you check your phone like it personally betrayed you.

Somewhere between the jokes, the late night talks, and the “I’m not hungry” that somehow became a full meal, we realized this wasn’t just a vibe. It was home.

Now we’re here, grateful, excited, and ready to celebrate with the people we love most. We can’t wait to share this day with you.
