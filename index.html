Our Story

It all started with three words: hey, wassup, hello.

Not poetry. Not some dramatic speech. Just three words that somehow changed everything.

Hey was simple. Wassup had confidence. Hello made sure the moment landed smoothly.

From that first exchange came laughs, late night talks, inside jokes, and the kind of connection you just know is different.

Now we get to celebrate this next chapter with all of you.
