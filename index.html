Our Story

It started with three words, and it turned into forever.

Three words. One legendary beginning.

It all started with a simple message: “Hey, wassup, hello.”

Not exactly the most polished pickup line, but it was enough to start a conversation. That conversation quickly turned into laughs, late-night talks, and a connection we couldn’t quite explain, effortless, constant, and meant to be.

Our first date on March 20, 2019 was simple but perfect, lunch and a Marvel movie. Our story began with a feeling neither of us could ignore.

From there, everything unfolded naturally. We’ve explored cities hand in hand and built a life together everywhere we’ve called home. Between the car rides, the unpacked boxes, the quiet nights in, and the unforgettable adventures, we found something that feels like magic, the kind you don’t question, just hold onto.

We’ve been together since May 28, 2019, got engaged on October 25, 2025, and now we can’t wait to say “I do” on October 10, 2026, stepping into our next chapter and celebrating with the people who mean the most to us. ✨

FAQ

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 4:30 PM so everyone is seated before the ceremony begins at 5:00 PM.

What should I wear?

We kindly ask women to wear floor-length gowns, elegant midi dresses, or dressy jumpsuits. For men, please wear a suit and tie or a tuxedo. One small request: please avoid wearing green.

Is parking available?

Yes. Parking will be available at the hotel. We recommend arriving a little early to allow time for parking and getting settled before the ceremony.

Are children invited?

To allow all guests to fully relax and enjoy the evening, we have chosen to make our wedding an adults-only celebration. We truly appreciate your understanding and can’t wait to celebrate with you.

Is there a hotel block?

Yes. Rooms have been reserved under The Gardner / Bully Wedding Block at Loews Atlanta Hotel, 1065 Peachtree St NE, Atlanta, GA 30309 .

Can I bring a plus-one?

We recommend booking as soon as possible so you can secure your room in the wedding block before availability runs out.
