Our Story

It all started with three words: “hey, wassup, hello.”

Not poetry. Not a Shakespeare monologue. Just three legendary words that somehow kicked off everything.

“Hey” was cautious. “Wassup” was bold. “Hello” was the smooth recovery because apparently one greeting just wasn’t enough.

Somewhere between the laughs, long talks, and “I’m not hungry” turning into sharing fries, something clicked.

Fast forward a few plot twists later — here we are planning a wedding and pretending we aren’t stressed with 47 tabs open.

And now we can’t wait to celebrate this next chapter with all of you.
